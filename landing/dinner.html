Why dinner?

A critic can tell you where to eat. A menu tells you what dinner might cost. A booking calendar answers the question that can undo both decisions: can you actually get a table?

Our sample replay holds the request in view as the pages change. The first restaurant makes sense until the calendar shows 10:15. Seeing the agent move on explains more about the task than watching it open ten tabs.

The replay uses fictional venues and availability. Its purpose is to show what a complete workflow should feel like.
