What counts as finished?

A shortlist can look convincing while overlooking the party size or quoting a lunch price for dinner. Checking the final answer alone can miss either mistake.

Our proposed evaluation records the request, the menu used to calculate the total, and the reservation slot selected. Each constraint gets checked against the same frozen set of pages so runs can be compared fairly.
